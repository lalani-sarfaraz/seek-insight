# seek-insight
RNA-seq Results Interpreter

Provide the top gene list results (in .csv format) from your DESeq2 comparisons, and sit back to "seek insights".
The app will provide you a preview of your input file, a summary of the up-/down-regulated genes in your data, \n
plot a volcano plot, provide lists of up- and down-regulated genes, perform pathway enrichment analysis, and \n
provide an LLM interpretation consisting of the following:
  1. Biological interpretation
  2. Potential pathways involved
  3. Potential technical concerns
  4. Suggested next analysis
  5. Concise manuscript-style summary

You could also ask your own questions to the LLM.


This tool can be accessed here:
https://seek-insight.streamlit.app

Happy insight seeking! :-)
