# seek-insight
**RNA-seq Results Interpretion Tool**

Provide the **<ins>top gene list results</ins>** (in **<ins>.csv</ins>** format) from your DESeq2 comparisons, and sit back to **"seek insights"**.
The app will:
  1. provide you a **<ins>preview of your input file</ins>**,
  2. a **<ins>summary of the up-/down-regulated genes</ins>** in your data,
  3. plot a **<ins>volcano plot</ins>**,
  4. provide **<ins>lists of up- and down-regulated genes</ins>**,
  5. perform **<ins>pathway enrichment analysis</ins>**, and
  6. provide an **<ins>LLM interpretation</ins>** consisting of the following:
       1) Biological interpretation,
       2) Potential pathways involved,
       3) Potential technical concerns,
       4) Suggested next analysis, and
       5) Concise manuscript-style summary

Additionally, you could also **<ins>ask your own questions</ins>** about the dataset to the LLM.


This tool can be accessed here:
https://seek-insight.streamlit.app

Happy insight seeking! :-)
